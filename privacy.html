Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Priya & Gautam Wedding Updates uses information collected through priyagautam.com, the wedding RSVP process, and the optional wedding text messaging service.

Information we collect

We may collect names, mobile numbers, invitation and RSVP details, event preferences, consent records, and wedding-related questions or messages. We may also collect basic technical information needed to maintain and secure the website and service.

How we use information

We use guest information to manage invitations and RSVPs, coordinate event logistics, send updates to guests who choose to receive texts, answer questions, and support the safety and reliability of these services. Information about an event may be restricted to guests invited to it.

Text messaging

Guests may optionally consent to recurring, non-promotional messages from Priya & Gautam Wedding Updates, including event reminders, location and transportation information, and schedule changes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not required to RSVP or attend.

Automated responses

Questions sent by text or through the website may be processed by an automated assistant. Messages may be processed by service providers that operate messaging, hosting, data storage, or AI services. Please do not send sensitive financial or medical information.

Sharing, retention, and security

We use service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the website, RSVPs, messaging, and guest assistance. We do not sell your personal information or SMS opt-in data to third parties. Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We limit access to authorized organizers and needed service providers, and retain information only as long as needed for wedding coordination, guest requests, consent documentation, and applicable obligations.

Your choices and contact

You may opt out of automated text messages at any time by replying STOP. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information, contact varpra1@gmail.com. We may retain limited records needed to honor opt-out preferences and recordkeeping obligations.
